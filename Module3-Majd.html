Explanation

ζ < 1: the response oscillates around its final value. The poles are a complex-conjugate pair and the phase trajectory spirals toward equilibrium.

ζ = 1: critical damping. This is the fastest non-oscillating response at a fixed natural frequency. The two real poles coincide.

ζ > 1: overdamping. Two distinct negative real poles give a slower, non-oscillating response.

Metrics: rise time is the interval from the first 10% crossing to the first 90% crossing. Peak time is the first maximum; it is not finite for a monotonic response. Settling time is the last entry into the ±2% band, found by searching the closed-form curve and refining the crossing. It is not the approximation 4/(ζωₙ).

Changing ωₙ: at fixed ζ it changes the time scale, but not the overshoot percentage. In this force-to-displacement model, changing k also changes K = 1/k; normalizing the main plot makes the timing easier to compare.
