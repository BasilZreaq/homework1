Module 4: Guess the Sensor. Identify a hidden first-order sensor from its measured step response.

Challenge controls

A hidden sensor receives a step input of amplitude A = 10. Estimate its gain K and time constant τ from the noisy observations.

Move the sliders to match the observed curve, then check your guess.

How to estimate

For a first-order sensor:

τ dy/dt + y = Kx

y(t) = KA(1 − e−t/τ)

Estimate K from the final output divided by A. Estimate τ from the time at which the response reaches about 63.2% of its final output. The measured dots include small deterministic noise.
